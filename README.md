## Why Overseas Citizenship of India Matters

The concept of OCI is crucial as it bridges the gap between full Indian citizenship and non-citizenship, offering a middle ground for those with Indian heritage living abroad. It grants various privileges, such as lifelong visa-free travel to India and the right to work in the country, without granting full citizenship rights. Understanding these nuances is vital for anyone looking to maintain a connection with India while living overseas.
